About NIMS

Where Nigeria's Industrial Workforce Comes Together

NIMS is a Presidential initiative dedicated to developing the skilled workforce needed to power Nigeria's industrial future. It brings together policymakers, business leaders, educators, investors and development partners to tackle the country's most pressing workforce challenges and create practical solutions that support industrial growth.

NIMS 2026

This year's summit builds on the momentum of the inaugural edition with a stronger focus on implementation, collaboration and measurable impact. Under the theme "Unlocking Nigeria's Human Capital for Industrial Transformation," NIMS 2026 will explore practical strategies for developing the workforce needed to support industrialisation, innovation and long-term economic prosperity.
